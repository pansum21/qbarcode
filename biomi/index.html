A few good questions

Before you make it yours.

What is BioMi?

BioMi brings your profile and your links into one public page. You build and edit it in a private dashboard, then publish it when you’re ready.

Do I need a separate account for every tool?

No. QBar Studio, BioMi and PixelMint share one account on qbarstudio.com. Sign in once in the same browser to move between the tools.

Can I change the colours and button shapes?

Yes. Your profile editor includes page and button colours, gradients, visual styles, photo shapes and rings. The interactive examples on this page are illustrations; your personal settings live in your dashboard.

How do I add more links?

Open Links in your dashboard, enter a topic and a URL, then save the link. Choose a scroll list or swipe pages with five links per page.

How do I use my page on Instagram or TikTok?

Publish your profile, copy its profile link, and paste it into the website or links field in your social account. This doesn’t require connecting your social account to BioMi.

Can other people see my draft?

Your dashboard requires sign-in. Visitors use your published profile link. Save and preview your work privately before publishing, and unpublish your public page when needed.

Will changing website Settings change my public profile?

Website Settings control the appearance of the QBar Studio tools. Your public BioMi page has its own theme controls inside the profile editor.

Can I use BioMi on my phone?

Yes. The dashboard has a mobile preview panel, and your profile supports phone, tablet and desktop layouts.
