About QBar Code Studio

QBar Code Studio is a free, web-based utility built to make creating scannable codes effortless, fast, and completely private. We support both two-dimensional QR codes (URLs, plain text, Wi-Fi, vCard) and linear retail and logistics barcodes (CODE128, CODE39, EAN-13, UPC-A, ITF-14, and Pharmacode).

Our Commitment to Privacy

Unlike many online tools that store user data or require registration, our processing is completely client-side. Your inputs never touch our backend databases, ensuring full data privacy.
