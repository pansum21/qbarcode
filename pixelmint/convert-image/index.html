COMMON QUESTIONS

Questions about this tool.

How do I convert JPG to PNG online?＋

Open the converter, choose a JPG image, select PNG, and process it. The original remains unchanged in your browser.

Can I convert PNG to JPG without uploading it?＋

Yes. PixelMint converts the image locally in your browser, so the file is not sent to a server.

How do I convert WebP to JPG?＋

Choose the WebP image, select JPG as the output format, then process and download the converted copy.

Will converting an image reduce its quality?＋

JPG and WebP quality can be adjusted. PNG keeps lossless image data, though the output file may be larger.
