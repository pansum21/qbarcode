COMMON QUESTIONS

Questions about this tool.

How do I make a passport size photo online?＋

Choose a portrait, select a passport or ID photo proportion, position the crop, and export the result.

Can I crop a photo for a visa or application form?＋

Use the available photo proportions to prepare a portrait crop for common passport, visa, and ID photo requirements. Always verify the exact rules with the receiving authority.

Does the passport photo maker upload my picture?＋

No. Cropping happens locally in your browser, and the image stays on your device.

Can I edit the crop after uploading?＋

Yes. Drag the portrait inside the frame to adjust its position before exporting.
