Before your first scan

A few things worth knowing.

Can I change the destination after downloading a QR?

These are static QR codes. The encoded content stays the same. If you need a new destination, generate a new code.

Can I scan the QR shown on this page?

Yes. The sample QR encodes https://qbarstudio.com/. Other decorative bars and animations are visual examples.

Will my code have branding?

QBarStudio.com is included below codes generated by QBar Studio, including supported exports and batch labels.

Can I use my logo and colours?

The QR styling tool supports colours, gradients and logo styling. Always scan-test your styled code before printing or distributing it.

Do all three tools use one account?

Yes. QBar Studio, BioMi and PixelMint share one account on qbarstudio.com in the same browser.

Where can I make multiple barcodes?

Use Bulk Barcodes & Labels to enter multiple values, generate printable labels and download the batch as a ZIP.
